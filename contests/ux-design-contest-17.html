Note From Adam

👋 Hi Designers,

As we launch Contest 17, I want to acknowledge the significant shifts in our industry. The past two years have brought unprecedented changes to the UX and Product Design landscape. With tech layoffs continuing to make headlines and UX design job openings down 71% from 2022 to 2023, it's clear we're still navigating challenging times.

At the same time, I've never been more optimistic about the future of designers.

Why? Because while traditional employment paths may have narrowed, the barriers to creating meaningful digital experiences have never been lower. The AI revolution isn't just changing how we work—it's democratizing the entire design and development process. Executing on design visions that once required teams of specialists are becoming accessible to individual creators. The gaps between idea, design, and functional product is shrinking rapidly.

This transformation presents an incredible opportunity for designers who are willing to adapt and innovate. Whether you're a seasoned professional looking to expand your toolkit or a newcomer ready to make your mark, there's never been a better time to experiment with new approaches and push the boundaries of what's possible for solopreneurs.

That's why this contest isn't just about showcasing your existing skills—-it's about exploring how emerging technologies can amplify your creative vision and help you deliver more value, faster than ever before.

I sure hope you'll join me for the fun ride of Contest 17. I can't wait to see what you're able to achieve.

Happy designing and good luck! 🤞

Adam Ruf
Creator, UX Design Contest

Project Brief

Mission

Create an impact-driven web site or app using AI to do the coding

Deliverables

  • Link to working website or application built using v0
  • Project pitch video (max. 5 minutes, link or file under 100MB)

Suggested Stages

  1. Problem/Opportunity
    • Identify an unmet need or challenge
    • Define target users and desired impact
  2. Solution Design
    • Leverage v0 to design and build your functional website or application
  3. Pitch Presentation
    • Introduce the problem/opportunity
    • Demonstrate your solution
    • Explain impact potential
    • Make a case for further investment

Desired Outcomes

By participating in this contest, you'll exercise important future-proof UX design skills, including:

  • Uncovering high-impact project ideas
  • Leveraging AI tools to make good decisions quickly
  • Moving beyond design mocks to deliver a functional, code-powered prototype
  • Presenting and pitching projects you want to work on

At the end, you'll have a real-world, zero-to-one project for your portfolio and a chance at winning money for your charity! 🏆🤞
